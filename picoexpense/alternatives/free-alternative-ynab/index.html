PicoExpense vs YNAB

YNAB (You Need A Budget) is a paid method-first planner: every dollar gets a job. PicoExpense is a local expense ledger with categories, budgets, and reports.

Feature PicoExpense YNAB
Cost Free — no app subscription Typically $14.99/mo or about $109/yr after a 34-day trial. Eligible students may get a longer trial. No permanent free plan.
Philosophy Record what happened. Monthly budgets are optional limits, not a required method. Zero-based: assign money you have before you spend it. The software exists to enforce that habit.
Where data lives IndexedDB in this browser. Optional file or Google Drive backup you control. YNAB’s cloud (commonly AWS). You sign in to use the product.
Bank sync None. Type it, import CSV, or draft from a local receipt scan. Optional linked accounts plus manual entry. Import is a core workflow.
Household One device (or restore a backup on another). No live shared budget. YNAB Together — typically up to six people on one subscription.
Offline First-class. Core tracking never needs the network. Apps exist, but the product is a service. Full use assumes an account and connectivity.
Reports This month, category mix, monthly/annual, budgets, tax flags. Age of money, spending, net worth-style views tied to the budget method.
Receipts Local PicoScan OCR; you still confirm before save. Not built as a receipt OCR product.
Credit cards Purchases are expenses; paying the card is a transfer, not new spending. Cards are first-class in the method (payment vs spending is taught explicitly).

Which should you choose?

Choose PicoExpense when…

  • You will not pay ~$100 a year for budgeting software
  • You do not want to hand bank logins to a budgeting company
  • You need the tracker to work on a flight, in another country, or with the radio off
  • You already know where money went and just need a private log
  • You are fine managing your own JSON backups

Choose YNAB when…

  • You want to be coached into zero-based budgeting every payday
  • You want bank import and a partner on the same live budget
  • The method (not just a list of expenses) is the product you are buying
  • You are willing to keep an account in the vendor’s cloud
