PicoExpense vs Spendee

Spendee focuses on wallets, budgets, and charts, with a free tier and Plus/Premium for bank sync and sharing. PicoExpense focuses on local entry, filters, and backups.

Which should you choose?

Choose PicoExpense when…

  • You do not need a live shared wallet with a partner in the cloud
  • You want every category and report without Plus
  • Desktop entry and a user guide in the app matter
  • Privacy means “not on Spendee’s servers”

Choose Spendee when…

  • Shared wallets with a partner are the feature you will use weekly
  • You like a highly visual mobile budget
  • You will pay Plus/Premium for bank connections
  • You are fine with a vendor account as the home of the data

Log spending without a shared-wallet plan

PicoExpense is for people who will type or import the row themselves — and keep the file.
