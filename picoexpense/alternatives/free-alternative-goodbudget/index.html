PicoExpense vs Goodbudget

Goodbudget is the well-known envelope app: fill envelopes, spend from them, sync a household. The free plan caps envelopes, accounts, history, and devices. PicoExpense has no such paywall.

Feature PicoExpense Goodbudget
Cost Free Free with limits; Premium commonly about $10/mo or $80/yr
Free-plan caps None on categories, accounts, or history in the app Typically 10 regular envelopes, one account, two devices, shorter history
Method Categories + optional monthly/annual budgets. Not envelope-strict. Envelope budgeting is the whole product

Transfers & cards Transfers and card payments are not expenses; card purchases are Envelopes plus accounts; you model cards inside the envelope rules

Which should you choose?

Choose PicoExpense when…

  • You hit Goodbudget’s free envelope or account cap
  • You do not want to pay Premium just to keep history or a second bank
  • You prefer categories and reports over filling envelopes every month
  • You want the data file on your machine

Choose Goodbudget when…

  • Envelopes are how you think, and you want the app to match that
  • A partner needs the same live envelope balances on their phone
  • You are happy inside a small free plan or will pay ~$80/yr
  • US bank sync on Premium is useful to you

Categories, not a paywalled envelope count

Add Food, Transport, and as many children as you need. Archive instead of deleting history.
