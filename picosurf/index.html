Just a small browser with a brain of its own and a secure helmet to shield data snooping.

Why PicoSurf?

Browsers became huge.

We wanted something smaller.

PicoSurf uses the operating system’s native WebView instead of shipping another complete browser engine. The result is a browser designed to stay lightweight, fast and focused.

Less software. Less baggage.

AI became cloud-dependent.

We wanted something different.

PicoSurf runs AI models locally on your device. When the browser needs AI to understand a page, summarize information or research the web, the intelligence can stay with you.

Your browsing context doesn’t need to become someone else’s data.

AI became a token meter.

Every question. Every page. Every API call. Tokens add up.

PicoSurf takes a different approach: download a suitable local model once and use it on your device.

No per-query AI bill.

A browser that can actually help you browse

Don’t just search. Tell PicoSurf what you’re looking for.

“Find the top 5 milk brands in UAE and compare them.”
“Find five good research papers about local LLM inference.”
“Open this page and summarize it.”
“Find the pricing on this website.”
“Compare these three products.”

PicoSurf can search, navigate, extract, compare and summarize — using local AI and the browser itself.

The browser remains the tool. AI just makes it smarter.

Your browser. Your AI. Your data.

PicoSurf is designed around a simple principle:

AI should work for you without requiring your data to work for someone else.

Local by default

Your AI interactions and saved knowledge can stay on this device.

Websites stay websites

Page scripts cannot read history, downloads, or local AI data.

Your browser remains yours

No account, no telemetry, no cloud LLM in the loop.

Small by design.

PicoSurf is part of the PicoAI philosophy:

Small software. Local intelligence. Private by default.

We don’t believe every application needs a cloud backend, a user account, a subscription or a massive runtime.

Sometimes, a small application running on your own machine is enough.
